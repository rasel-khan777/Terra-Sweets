Guwahati's Architectural Renaissance

A Culinary Sanctuary Above the Valley: The V4.0 Metamorphosis

Elevated six storeys above the vibrant arterial pulse of GS Road, Terra Mayaa introduces an African-inspired earthiness reimagined with clean Nordic lines and indigenous bamboo warmth. A sensory destination where Assam's river mist meets world-class gastronomy.

Curated under the culinary patronage of culinary innovators Chef Priyam Chatterjee and Chef Rishabh Seal, each plate celebrates Neo-Indian imagination—fusing heritage Assamese ingredients with progressive sous-vide and charcoal smoke techniques.
