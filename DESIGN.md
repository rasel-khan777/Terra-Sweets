## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital environment for a premier rooftop restaurant, microbrewery, and lounge nestled overlooking the Brahmaputra valley. The aesthetic merges modern luxury with coastal-toned minimalism: airy open spaces, crisp architectural lines, and timeless hospitality cues.

### Personality & Tone
- **Sophisticated & Poised:** Elevated hospitality without pretension. High-contrast typography paired with generous, deliberate whitespace.
- **Atmospheric & Refreshing:** Deep maritime blues evoke sunset rooftop vistas, balanced by chilled ice blues and pure whites that simulate breezy open-air dining.
- **Artisanal & Refined:** Polished brass and champagne gold touches convey the craftsmanship of artisanal brewing, fine culinary plating, and curated cocktail culture.

### Visual Style
The design system combines **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with subtle tactile depth. It avoids heavy skew or aggressive flat patterns, instead opting for hairline borders, whisper-soft atmospheric shadows tinted in twilight blue, and high-elegance transitional serif headlines supported by geometric humanist UI typography.

## Colors

The palette balances clean architectural light backgrounds with oceanic blues and warm metallics, evoking open sky and dusk dining.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#285B8E`):** Oceanic Blue. Serves as the primary brand anchor, interactive button fill, active tab indicators, and featured link states.
- **Secondary (`#142B45`):** Deep Twilight Navy. Delivers high-contrast typography, luxury banner backdrops, footer panels, and prominent editorial anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#C6A66A`):** Champagne Gold. Applied selectively to exclusive badges, VIP reservations, brewery award markers, and star ratings. Never used for large body copy.
- **Neutral Core (`#394C60`):** Polished Slate. Governs primary body text and metadata, offering soft contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - `Background`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F4F8FC` (Ice Blue for alternating sections and table head rows)
  - `Surface Highlight`: `#EAF2FA` (Soft Blue for elevated card fills and calendar selectors)
  - `Surface Border / Hairline`: `#E1E9F1` (Crisp delineation for inputs, tables, and card outlines)
  - `Muted Slate`: `#718096` (Tertiary captions, placeholder text, and inactive borders)

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects curated hospitality: editorial transitional serifs paired with structured, legible modern grotesques.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for page headings, section milestones, signature dish titles, and cocktail narratives. It brings warmth, literary prestige, and high culinary presence.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean readability for menu descriptions, guest reservation inputs, ingredient lists, and operational UI.
- **Bilingual & Data Fallbacks:** In multilingual or Assamese-specific contextual instances, pair styling with `Noto Sans Assamese` matching the weight scale of Manrope. Tabular wine lists and financial tallies may deploy `Inter` or tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum width of `1280px`. Whitespace is treated as a structural material, signaling exclusivity and room to breathe.

### Grid Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` (20px) and `gutter-mobile` (16px). Cards stack vertically; horizontal scroll is permitted strictly for chef’s specials and date-picker pills.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (24px) and adaptive margins (32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid with `margin` (48px) and `gutter` (24px), providing room for multi-column culinary menus, paired beverage listings, and side-by-side booking flows.

### Rhythm
Components follow an 8px base rhythm. Section dividers rely on broad vertical separation (`space-xxl`) combined with delicate 1px `#E1E9F1` rules rather than heavy graphical blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, light-diffused atmospheric shadows that mimic daylight falling across rooftop cabanas, blended with hairline boundaries.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface:** Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F8FC`.
2. **Elevated Panels (Menu Cards, Table Cards):**
   - Hairline border: `1px solid #E1E9F1`.
   - Shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.04), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive & Floating Elements (Reservation Floating Bar, Cart Summary, Dropdowns):**
   - Hairline border: `1px solid rgba(225, 233, 241, 0.8)`.
   - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Overlays:**
   - Backdrop: `rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.
   - Modal Container: Solid `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E1E9F1`, elevated with `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Level 2 Roundedness** (8px default border radius, extending to 12px and 16px on larger cards), creating a tactile, polished feel that avoids both austere razor corners and overly playful pill contours.

- **Default UI Elements (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Buttons, text fields, menu badges, and dropdown menus.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 12px):** Dining course cards, tasting menus, booking dates, and promotional callouts.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl` / 16px):** Reservation dialogs, floor-plan previews, and full-bleed image containers.
- **Status Pills (`rounded-full`):** Category filter chips and VIP/Microbrewery badges where continuous organic shapes convey fluidity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#285B8E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 8px, padding `12px 24px`. Hover state shifts to `#366FA6` with smooth 200ms transition. Active scale down to 0.98.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, text `#285B8E`, border `1px solid #285B8E`. Hover fills with `#F4F8FC`.
- **Luxury / Accent:** Background `#C6A66A`, text `#FFFFFF`, used strictly for "Reserve Table" or "Exclusive Tasting" VIP CTAs. Hover shifts to `#B59458`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E1E9F1`, text `#394C60`, border-radius 8px, padding `12px 16px`. Placeholder text set to `#718096`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#285B8E`, subtle outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(40, 91, 142, 0.12)`.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` uppercase (`#142B45`) with letter-spacing `0.06em`.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Culinary Item Card:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a `1px solid #E1E9F1` border. Features an image container with an 8px radius, an item title in `headline-sm` (`#142B45`), and pricing aligned right in tabular Manrope. Dietary symbols (vegan, microbrew signature) sit as unobtrusive 18px icons.
- **Highlight Card (Brewery & Special Events):** Subtle `#F4F8FC` surface with an accent line or champagne badge pinned to the top right corner.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `8px 16px` padding. Inactive state: background `#F4F8FC`, text `#394C60`, border `1px solid transparent`. Active state: background `#285B8E`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **VIP & Tasting Badges:** Background `rgba(198, 166, 106, 0.12)`, text `#C6A66A`, border `1px solid rgba(198, 166, 106, 0.3)`.

### Reservation & Table Status Indicators
- **Available:** Dot in soft turquoise/blue (`#285B8E`), text in `#394C60`.
- **Reserved / Waitlist:** Soft gold dot (`#C6A66A`).
- **Unavailable:** Muted grey (`#E1E9F1`).
